The Pizza Sales Analytics Dashboard analyzes pizza sales performance, customer ordering behaviour, revenue trends, product demand, and order patterns.

The dashboard is designed to help business management understand overall sales performance, identify high-performing pizza categories and products, and discover opportunities to improve inventory planning and sales strategies.

🎯 Objectives

The dashboard aims to:

Analyze total revenue and total orders.
Calculate the average order value.
Determine the average number of pizzas ordered.
Identify the highest-selling pizza.
Analyze revenue by pizza size.
Compare monthly order trends.
Identify low-performing sales periods.
Understand customer ordering patterns.
📊 Dashboard Components
KPI Summary Cards

The dashboard highlights the following key findings:

Total Revenue: $329K
Total Orders: 9K
Average Order Value: $38.40
Average Pizza per Order: 2.33

These KPI cards provide a quick overview of the overall sales performance.

Monthly Order Trends

The dashboard compares pizza orders across different months.

Key findings include:

January recorded the highest monthly order volume.
December recorded the lowest monthly order volume.

The difference in monthly performance highlights potential opportunities for seasonal promotions during lower-performing periods.

Top-Selling Pizza

The product performance analysis identifies:

Top-Selling Pizza: The Thai Chicken Pizza

This indicates strong customer demand for this product and suggests that it should receive attention in inventory planning and promotional campaigns.

Revenue by Pizza Size

The dashboard compares revenue generated across different pizza sizes.

Large pizzas generated the highest revenue.

This suggests that pizza size is an important factor influencing sales value and should be considered when designing product promotions and pricing strategies.

📈 Key Trends Discovered
Total revenue reached $329K.
The business processed approximately 9K orders.
The average order value was $38.40.
Customers purchased an average of 2.33 pizzas per order.
January recorded the highest order volume.
December recorded the lowest order volume.
The Thai Chicken Pizza was the top-selling product.
Large pizzas generated the highest revenue.
💡 Recommendations
Promote top-selling pizzas to maintain strong demand.
Use product bundles to increase the average number of pizzas per order.
Introduce targeted promotions during lower-performing months, especially December.
Prioritize inventory planning for high-demand pizzas.
Develop marketing campaigns around large pizzas because they generate the highest revenue.
Analyze seasonal demand patterns to improve sales forecasting.
📌 Conclusion

The Pizza Sales Analytics Dashboard provides valuable insight into revenue performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product demand, and seasonal sales trends.

The analysis shows that January has the strongest order performance, while December presents an opportunity for targeted promotions. The Thai Chicken Pizza is the top-selling product, and Large pizzas generate the highest revenue.

These insights can support better decisions around inventory planning, product promotion, pricing, bundling, and seasonal sales strategies.
